260. **`Fs` has no way to make a write reach the disk before it answers.** `Fs.write` is the host's `file:write_file`, which calls no sync, and E.17 promises of `rename` what a reader of the path sees, not what a power loss leaves. A program that keeps data across a power loss cannot be written in Ernest. The host has `file:sync/1` and `file:datasync/1`, so E.0 rule 1 admits the operation and rule 4 does not refuse it: the question is its shape. Two facts for it: `Fs` has paths and no open file, where the host's sync takes an open file; and a write that survives needs the file synced and, after the rename, its directory. Found 2026-11-17 by a reading of the guide's kept count (the log's *The Outside Reviews Read*); its shape is decided in a design discussion of its own, before MVP 4.4's release.
