# Findings at the limits, 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-17

A reading of the decisions log since Ernest 0.10.0, for one question: did like findings at the limits get like answers and like effort?

It is the record of one reading, made on 2026-11-17, and is not kept current. The log owns each finding, and the sorting under *Whose* and *Kind* is this reading's own. What it left to decide is MVP 4.16's item 101 (the log's *The Limits Read*).

The short answer. The answers follow the cause, and most are consistent. A test that read an order nothing promises was changed, every time. A limit of the host's was stated, or moved where no program can see its outcome, by rules each entry names. What is not consistent is smaller: when an open order gets a sentence, which never-failed tests are changed, which host defects get a report, and what a slow target of the repository's own is worth.

The effort is not consistent across the week. It fell on 2026-11-16, at the rule for a hunt, and the measuring items of that evening did not follow it.

## What was read

- The seven log files as the working tree has them. `docs/log/2026-11-17.md` held eight lines not yet committed when it was read. Entries were picked by their headings, by the plan's table and by a search for words of load, race, signal and limit.
- Read whole: every entry the table names, but those below.
- Read in part: *A Package and Its Manifest* (one sentence, row 12), *The Review's Feedback Decided* (feedback 249 alone), *The Named Packages Built* (one paragraph), *The Trust Said Once* (its opening and the paragraphs on a pipe and the window).
- `docs/plan.md`'s MVP 4.16, and the opening lines of OTP's reports 19 to 23 in `docs/otp_bugs.md`.

Left out, as findings about rules on an ordinary program: *A Compiled Module the Host Cannot Load* but for the limit it found, *A Given Path Resolved Once*, *The Ownership Test Built*, `Http`'s first line, and feedback 261, a listener handed over.

Four rows are at the edge and are marked in the table: the deadlock's scope (11), which came from a proposal, and three slow tools (44, 45, 46), which no failure showed.
