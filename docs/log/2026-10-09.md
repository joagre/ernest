## The Canonical Form, Built, 2026-10-09

MVP 3.1's item 2, built to [`mvp3.1.md`](../proposals/nodes_and_code/mvp3.1.md)'s section 6 and [`code.md`](../proposals/nodes_and_code/code.md)'s section 2, the test suite before the code. What the building decided:

- **The form is a normative appendix, H, of the toolchain's file.** The plan had a document of its own; the form is a rule a conforming toolchain must meet, and a rule lives in the report. Its placement after Appendix C keeps every number.
- **The bytes are the host's external term format, deterministic, of a term with its version inside**, `{ernest_form, 1, Form}`, SHA-256 of `term_to_binary(Term, [deterministic, {minor_version, 2}])`. The host documents the format and keeps it across a major release, which is the floor (§8.7); the term holds no map, so its bytes follow from the format's rules alone, and another toolchain writing the same terms computes the same hashes. - **What the standard library, the prelude and a foreign declaration are referenced by is their qualified name**, inside the standard library's own build too, since the floor covers what the hashes leave out and a program's hash must not change with a patch of the library it stands on.
- **A group member's hash is one value**, SHA-256 of `{ernest_member, 1, GroupHash, Position}`, so every definition's identity is a 32-byte binary; a lambda's or a local function's identity is the pair of its definition's hash and its position in the walk, since nothing names a lambda alone yet.
- **An abstract type's hash covers its whole declaration**, a value crossing with its representation; whether a declaration is exported, or a type abstract, is not in its form.
- **An annotation is in the form, resolved.** A body written with and without an annotation is two definitions, since an annotation changes inference, a rigid variable among it; layout, comments, names of locals and of annotation variables, and a pattern's field order are not.
- **Three inferred types are in the form, and no other**: an operator's operand type where the runtime applies the operator at it, the sum type `<-` unwraps, and a call's answer type, each because the emitted code reads it at the node and nothing else in the form fixes it; a pipe whose callee is computed is its own node, since §5.1 orders its evaluation otherwise. - **§11.1's recompile rule changed.** A change confined to a dependency's bodies used to leave its dependents' `.erc` files alone; a dependent's hashes now depend on its dependencies' hashes through its forms, so the build recompiles a dependent whose forms reference a changed hash, and the cascade follows the hashes. - **A user's module keeps the host's debug information**, since `make dialyzer` reads it from the library's modules, which `ern build` compiles like any; an installation strips it and the installation test holds that. The principles review's L40 is closed by it.
- **The forms' chunk is compressed**, decided with the user: 678 KB uncompressed over the standard library and the libraries, half their compiled size and eight times their code, against 81 KB compressed. `ern_chunk` reads a compressed term as data alone and refuses one whose declared size exceeds 1032 times its compressed size, deflate's own maximum ratio, a bound derived from the format and not a number chosen; the hash is of the uncompressed bytes.
