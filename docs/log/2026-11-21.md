## A Busy Loop in a Test, 2026-11-21

Found by the reading before the clear of the session that closed MVP 4.16's item 93, and decided on the user's word: busy loops are forbidden.

**What stood.** Item 93's test in Ernest of a program whose last act is `closeListener` held an accept on the listener first. To know that the accept waited, it polled `Process.status` in a loop until the acceptor showed a call. The session read the agent's diff and let the loop stand. The same loop stands in a session typed into a shell in `test/ern_nodes_tests.erl`, older than the item.

**Why it is a defect and a gap.** A process waits on what it means, and a loop that asks until the answer changes waits on nothing. Nothing in the library waits until another process waits, so the loop is written around a gap, which nothing may be. The repository's Erlang tests wait so by the host's trace, in `ern_waits`.

**What was done.** The new test is made without the held accept: its program listens and closes, and a close that waits on a held accept, with the runtime's look during it, stays `ern_tcp_tests`'. The older site is feedback 269 and the plan's item 156, in MVP 4.5, since only a test meets it: a wait of `Test`'s weighed on E.0's admission rules, or the test made without it. It is weighed with that milestone's item 5, feedback 264, a loop with a sleep over `Peer.utilizations`.
