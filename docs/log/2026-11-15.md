## The Shell's Last Line, 2026-11-15

MVP 4.16's item 40 (*The Flake Hunt*). The terminal-gone tests of `ern_terminal_tests` failed now and then under `make test-shell` and `make test-full`. A put was unmet, or a test ended 0 where 129 was due, or the shell did not say `the shell ends: its terminal is gone`. There were three causes, one in `ern` and two in the harness.

**The line before the signal.** A job ends by the hangup's signal once its output is flushed (§11.8). `ern` sent itself the signal first and wrote its streams out after. A stream found gone at the end also ended the job before the other was written. The host writes a descriptor's port from a thread of its own, so a write it has answered may not be written yet. A signal the job sends itself loses that write, and `halt` does not. So the shell's line on standard error was lost now and then, though the status was right.

**Both streams first.** The end now writes both streams out, each whatever the other's state, and then ends. It ends by the signal that ended the program, which stands over a stream found gone (§8.6). Otherwise it ends by the first stream found gone, and otherwise with the status. The report did not change.

**The harness's two faults.** `Os.closeInput` is a send (E.23), and the hangup step was met as soon as it was sent. So the put after it could reach the program before its terminal was hung up, and the program ended 0. The step is now met once a write of nothing after it answers `Left(Closed)`, which the program's process gives only once the terminal is hung up. A put was also unmet where the program's end reached the harness before the putter's answer. The put is what ends these programs, and the two answers come by two ways, so a put now waits for its own answer through the end.

**The proof.** Hooks that only delay, in scratch copies, forced each order. They delayed the standard error write by 50 ms, the helper's hangup by 20 ms and the putter's answer by 300 ms. The old code and harness failed in every forced run, 12, 12 and 18, with the symptoms seen, and the new passed in all. With 96 runs at once, the four tests failed 6 times in 1,344 runs before and none in 2,304 after. `finished_beside_a_gone_stream_test` is a regression test, written after the code. It forces the end to find a stream gone and holds that the other is written and the signal stands. It does not cover the loss itself, which is a race of the host's thread.

**Not found.** `shell_reload_terminal_gone` once ended 0, which none of the three causes explains. It was not reproduced, and its line is kept as the plan's item 67.

**What the end outside a run still does.** A stream found gone outside a run ends the job at once, and so does a signal there, with the other stream not written out. A device's exit between a run's end and the end's wait takes the same path. §11 says "at once" of both. Whether a line the job has said on the other stream is then written is a reading for the user, the plan's item 68. It is read from the code, and no program was run that shows a line lost.

**What the hunt met besides.** `load_beside_a_module_hiding_a_library`, the plan's item 61, failed in 2 of 12 runs of the shell's area before the change and in none of 13 after. One run of `ern_cli_tests` failed one test of 408 whose name was not kept, and ten runs after it passed.
